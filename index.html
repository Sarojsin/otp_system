How it works

  1. Enter your email or phone number
  2. Click "Send OTP" to receive a 6-digit code
  3. Enter the code in the verification section
  4. Click "Verify OTP" to complete authentication

Note: OTPs expire in 5 minutes and have 3 attempt limits
